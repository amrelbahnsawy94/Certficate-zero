What it does

Previous quantity, percentage and amount take the old cumulative figures. Current figures become zero. Cumulative is unchanged. Formulas for totals, retention, advance recovery, VAT and variations are never overwritten.

What it leaves to you

Certificate number, period and dates, lists of previous payments, rates and contract sum changes. The tool lists these cells so none is forgotten. It does not value work or certify anything.
